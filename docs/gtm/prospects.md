# CloseCheck — GTM prospect list (first pass)

**Date built: 2026-09-22.** Built per `docs/research/promotion.md` §3 and
`docs/research/distribution.md` ("first ten" plan): a named list of firms/consultants across the two
target populations, assembled entirely from public sources in one research session.

**Populations:**
- **A — existing technology-DD providers** (they already sell tech/IT/website due diligence; pitch =
  tool-switch: do it faster/cheaper/whitelabeled with CloseCheck).
- **B — QoE generalists and IT/fractional-CIO consultants without a tech-DD line** (pitch = new
  service line: add a tech-diligence workstream to your existing engagement).

**Seed sources used:** third-party QoE comparison guides (ctacquisitions.com "18 Firms Ranked",
thewebsiteflip.com, projectionhub.com, smash.vc, thevisualcommunicationguy.com, investing.io),
publicly self-promoting SMB QoE firm sites, the Self-Funded Search Conference sponsor list (Nov 7–9
2026, Dallas), Acquiring Minds webinar guest roster, Searchfunder posts, VerSquare provider
directory, Flippa's DD partner directory, IBBA Supplier Directory, AM&AA 2026 Summer Conference
agenda, and SERP sweeps for "technology due diligence" / "IT due diligence M&A".

**Confidence key:** **H** = firm site fetched directly this session (services/people/contact
verified); **M** = named consistently across 2+ independent public sources, or one direct source
with partial detail; **L** = single secondary mention, not independently verified.

**Rule followed on contacts:** no emails are guessed or constructed. An email appears only where the
firm publishes it on its own site. Otherwise the channel is recorded as "contact form" or
"LinkedIn". Contact-person names come only from public team pages, podcast/webinar appearances, or
public LinkedIn profiles.

**Deliberately excluded:** Big 4 / national TAS practices from the ctacquisitions guide (EY,
Deloitte, PwC, KPMG, RSM, BDO, Grant Thornton — $75K+ engagements, wrong segment); AI-DD software
vendors (DEALPRINT, Devaland, Finsider.ai, Bizzed — competitors, not customers, per
`docs/research/competitors-2026-09.md`); DueDilio (platform risk, per `distribution.md`; the Beylin
newsletter touch is tracked in `promotion.md`, not here); pure lenders/insurers/law firms from the
SFSC sponsor list (Live Oak Bank, Symphony Risk, Steckler Wayne & Love, Commercial Lending X, Aspen
HR — channel contacts, not prospects).

## Which seed sources were most / least productive

**Most productive:**
1. **Acquiring Minds webinar guest roster** — single highest-value page found: surfaced both
   bulls-eye Population A prospects (Preferred Data Corp's "Quality of Technology" practice, Inzo
   Technologies) plus named partners at Cayne Crossing and LCS. Nobody else had these names.
2. **Third-party comparison guides, but the SMB-native ones** — thewebsiteflip.com, projectionhub,
   smash.vc, investing.io, thevisualcommunicationguy: ~15 correctly-sized Population B firms with
   pricing and founder names.
3. **Flippa's DD partner directory** — 6 named online-business DD firms (WebAcquisition, Jaded,
   Centurica, Rapid Diligence, CapForge, Eyeful Media) in one page.
4. **SERP sweep for "technology due diligence" terms** — produced most of Population A, though skewed
   enterprise; confirms `promotion.md` §4's finding that nobody serves the SMB angle.
5. **SFSC sponsor list** — small but high-warmth: Cayne Crossing, System Six, LCS (via association)
   will physically be at the Nov 7–9 Dallas conference.
6. **VerSquare provider directory** — new discovery this session: review-ranked searcher-service
   directory (87 diligence providers listed; only top-ranked names visible without login — worth a
   deeper manual pass).

**Least productive:**
1. **ctacquisitions' "18 Firms Ranked" guide** — the marquee seed source was a dud for this purpose:
   all 18 firms are Big 4 / national / large regional ($50K+ engagements, wrong segment). Its
   *publisher* (CT Acquisitions) made the list instead.
2. **IBBA Supplier Directory** — almost entirely CRM/valuation software vendors and lenders; one
   usable prospect (BizWorth). Confirms it as a *listing venue* for CloseCheck, not a prospect mine.
3. **AM&AA 2026 Summer Conference agenda** — 23 named speakers, zero from DD/QoE/tech-advisory
   firms (PE, legal, insurance). The association path `distribution.md` budgeted weeks for remains
   low-yield, as `promotion.md` predicted.
4. **Searchfunder QoE-referral threads** — providers deliberately unnamed/redacted in public posts;
   consistent with the DueDilio anonymization finding.

## Notes for outreach sequencing

- Nine firms have a **published email**; everyone else is contact-form or LinkedIn — consistent with
  running `distribution.md`'s LinkedIn-vs-email A/B on matched pairs.
- The **Dallas cluster** (Cayne Crossing, System Six, LCS, plus any diligence-panel speakers
  announced later) should be sequenced so first contact lands 2–3 weeks before SFSC (Nov 7–9).
- **Bedrock QoE** is the highest-leverage single relationship: prospect + Acquisitions Anonymous
  (Girdley) + SMB-tax Twitter (Baldridge) in one firm.
- Population A enterprise names (#11–12, 28–30) are recorded to bound the population, not for launch
  outreach.
